Privacy Policy

SmartCalc – Exact Calculator · Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains how the SmartCalc calculator app ("the app"), published by Abubakar Hajj ("we"), handles information. The short version: the app does not collect, send or share any personal data.

Information the app stores

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • your calculation history (the calculations and their results),
  • your current, unfinished calculation,
  • your settings: light or dark theme, degrees or radians, whether the scientific keys are open,
  • whether you have already seen the introduction screens.

The app never sends this information anywhere. We do not have a server and cannot see it. If you have turned on Android's own backup for your phone, Android may include this information in your personal device backup in your Google account, as it does for other apps. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; we cannot access it.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not send any data over the internet.

Permissions

The app uses the vibration permission to give a light touch feedback when you press keys. The app also lists the internet permission, which is included by the framework the app is built with; the app does not use it to send or receive any of your information. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Clipboard

When you tap an answer, the app copies that number to your device's clipboard. It does not read your clipboard.

Deleting your data

You can delete your history at any time in the app (History → Clear history). Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything the app stored.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, this policy will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change.
